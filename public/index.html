🌟 The Legend of Move 37

AlphaGo vs. Lee Sedol — Game 2, Move 37 (March 10, 2016)

At P10 on the 19x19 board, AlphaGo played a move that sent shockwaves across the globe and forever altered our understanding of artificial intelligence and game theory.

  • Breaking 2,500 Years of Dogma: In classical Go, playing a shoulder hit on the 5th line was considered unthinkable. Human dogma dictated that you only play shoulder hits on the 3rd or 4th lines—playing on the 5th line pushes the opponent along the 4th line, conceding massive, uncontested territory on the side.
  • The 1-in-10,000 Probability: Fan Hui and the DeepMind research team analyzed AlphaGo's neural networks after the match. AlphaGo calculated that a human professional would choose this move only 1 time in 10,000 (0.01%). Yet its value network evaluated that this move gave it the highest winning probability.
  • Lee Sedol's 15-Minute Walkout: Lee Sedol froze in astonishment. Unable to fathom what he had just seen, he stood up, left the room into the hotel corridor, and spent nearly 15 minutes regaining composure before playing Move 38.
  • Cosmic Thickness: AlphaGo gave away fourth-line side territory to weave a majestic diagonal highway of stones through the center, linking disparate groups into an unassailable sphere of cosmic influence.
  • True AI Creativity: Move 37 proved that deep neural networks were not merely memorizing human knowledge—they had transcended human blind spots to reveal sublime beauty.

✨ Move 78: "The Divine Move" (God's Touch)

Lee Sedol vs. AlphaGo — Game 4, Move 78 (March 13, 2016)

Down 0-3 in the match and facing seemingly insurmountable despair, legendary 9-dan Lee Sedol produced humanity's greatest tactical triumph.

  • The Wedge into the Void: Lee Sedol struck at K10, wedging a white stone directly between AlphaGo's two impenetrable center walls.
  • AlphaGo's Blind Spot: AlphaGo calculated that the probability of a human finding this exact move was under 0.007%. Because its Monte Carlo tree search pruned branches it deemed virtually impossible, it was unprepared for the complex tactical ripple effects.
  • The Hallucination Cascade: AlphaGo's win-rate curve plummeted from 70% to under 20% within several moves, leading to erratic play and Lee Sedol's unforgettable victory for humanity.
